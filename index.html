Quality

Every Meter Gets Checked

No third-party certification yet? We're honest about that. Here's what we do instead.

  • Incoming yarn inspection — we verify fiber composition and denier before weaving starts. Bad yarn = bad fabric. We catch it early.
  • Loom-level QC — every roll is checked for weaving defects (broken ends, missing picks, stains) before it leaves the loom floor.
  • Post-finishing inspection — after dyeing, coating, or laminating, each batch is spot-checked for colorfastness, coating adhesion, and hand feel consistency.
  • Pre-shipment random sampling — before your order leaves, we pull random meters, measure against your spec, and photograph the results. You get the report before you pay the balance.

If your project requires OEKO-TEX, REACH, or other formal certifications, we can arrange third-party testing. Tell us upfront and we'll include it in the quote.
